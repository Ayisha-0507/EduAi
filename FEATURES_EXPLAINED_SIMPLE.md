# Simple Explanations for Judges

---

## Text Summarizer
The Text Summarizer lets you paste a long chapter or any text, and the AI will turn it into short, easy-to-read bullet points, flashcards, exam notes, or even a mind map. It helps you quickly understand and revise big topics without reading everything word for word.

**How to explain:**
“Paste any textbook or notes, and the AI gives you a summary, flashcards, or exam notes in seconds. It saves time and makes studying easier.”

---

## Feynman Board
The Feynman Board is based on a famous learning technique: if you can teach something simply, you really understand it. Here, you explain a topic to the AI, and the AI acts like a curious student, asking questions. If you get stuck, you know what to revise. It helps you find and fix gaps in your understanding.

**How to explain:**
“You teach the AI a topic, and it asks you questions like a student. If you can answer, you truly understand. If not, you know what to study more.”

---

## Flashcards
The Flashcard feature creates quick question-and-answer cards from any topic you type. You can use these cards to test yourself and revise fast. It’s like having your own set of digital study cards made by AI.

**How to explain:**
“Type a topic, and the AI makes flashcards for you. You can flip through them to test yourself and remember things better.”
